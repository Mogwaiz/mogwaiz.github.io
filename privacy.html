Privacy policy

Fitbit Diary is used only by its owner (mogwaiz@gmail.com). It is not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed: read-only Google Health data (activity, workouts and GPS routes, sleep, heart rate and other health measurements) from the owner's own account. Nothing is written to Google.

Storage: data is stored only on the owner's private home server. It is not sold, shared or published.

Processing: calculated workout figures are sent to Amazon Bedrock (EU) to write short diary notes for the owner.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: mogwaiz@gmail.com
